work

What I'm building.

A clustering model can find patterns in seismic data. An interpreter also needs to know what a pattern means, and why. My research links what the model sees to what the geological literature says, so that every interpretation can be traced back to a concept and a source.

  • 01 · OntoGeoRAG

    Retrieval guided by a knowledge graph

    A retrieval and LLM pipeline for interpreting mass transport deposits, with citable, geology-grounded explanations.

  • 02 · Representations

    Seismic images meet the literature

    Joint text and image representations of seismic patches, clustered with HDBSCAN and KMeans, then mapped to geologic descriptors.

  • 03 · Explainability

    Predictions you can trace

    Interpretations linked to the knowledge graph, from a prediction back to geological concepts and literature sources.

The pipeline is open source, with versioned releases on GitHub and Zenodo: github.com/feriel21/ontogeorag-cageo
